Problem, Setup, and Research Direction

The first page frames the research question: how to keep semantic segmentation reliable when clear weather training no longer matches fog, rain, snow, and night test conditions.

Dataset

ACDC adverse weather images are used to evaluate semantic segmentation under realistic urban distribution shift.

Baseline

SegFormer provides the segmentation baseline and the intermediate stage features for adaptation analysis.

Direction

Feature alignment and DINOv3 semantic priors are used to improve robustness without fully retraining the segmentation model.
